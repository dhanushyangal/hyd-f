Luma is often video, NeRF, or Gaussian splats in public perception. Hydrilla is a mesh with PBR maps you export into DCC tools and game engines. They overlap on the phrase “AI 3D” and diverge on the file you take home.

## Side by side

| | Hydrilla | Luma |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core job | Text/image → segmented mesh + PBR | Capture and generative 3D (see Luma’s current product) |
| Model | BlueFox 1 (Hawan Research Labs) | Luma’s models |
| Typical output | Mesh you open in Blender, Unity, Unreal | Often splats, video, or capture — confirm on Luma |
| Exports we ship | GLB, FBX, OBJ, USDZ | Confirm on Luma’s site |
| Studio | Hydrilla workspaces and credits | Luma’s apps |

## How to decide

If the next person to touch the file is an Unreal or Blender artist, test Hydrilla on a real prop. If your pipeline is built around Luma capture or splats, stay there unless you need BlueFox’s mesh-and-PBR handoff.
