Hydrilla is the product around BlueFox 1, built by Hawan Research Labs. Meshy is a widely used AI 3D generator with its own models and web app. We do not claim Hydrilla is “better quality.” We claim a named lab, a named model, production exports, and a studio workflow.

## Side by side

| | Hydrilla | Meshy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Studio product + BlueFox 1 model | Meshy’s own app and models |
| Who builds the model | Hawan Research Labs | Meshy |
| Inputs | Text prompt, reference image | Text and image (plus Meshy-specific tools) |
| Segmentation | Logical parts on the mesh | See Meshy |
| PBR | Base color, metalness, roughness, normals | See Meshy |
| Exports | GLB, FBX, OBJ, USDZ | Check Meshy’s current list |
| Who it is for | Creators, studios, production-ready meshes — not CAD | See Meshy’s positioning |

Meshy’s prices, formats, and features change. Verify on their site.

You want BlueFox 1 via the Hydrilla studio, in-browser preview, and files that enter Unity, Unreal, or Blender. Start on the [free plan](/pricing).
