Hydrilla and Tripo both turn text or images into 3D meshes. Hydrilla is the studio around BlueFox 1, with preview, credits, workspaces, and GLB, FBX, OBJ, and USDZ export. Tripo is a separate generator with its own models and plans. We do not claim a quality score.

## Side by side

| | Hydrilla | Tripo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | BlueFox 1 (Hawan Research Labs) | Tripo’s models |
| Inputs | Text, image | Text, image (see Tripo for extras) |
| PBR / segmentation | PBR maps and logical parts | Confirm on Tripo |
| Exports | GLB, FBX, OBJ, USDZ | Confirm on Tripo |
| Who it is for | Creators and studios, production-ready meshes, not CAD | See Tripo |
| Free tier | Yes, 200 credits/month, GLB | See Tripo |

Feature lists drift. Re-check Tripo before an internal bake-off.
