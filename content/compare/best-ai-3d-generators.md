The category is AI 3D generators for production assets — files meant to enter a game engine, DCC, or viz pipeline, not only a web preview. Hydrilla (BlueFox 1) belongs next to Meshy, Luma, and Tripo because those are the names people already search. There is no single winner without a bake-off in your scene.

## What criteria actually matter?

Text and image input. Export formats. Production mesh vs research preview. Team workflow (workspaces, credits, seats). Who the product is for.

Hydrilla is built for creators, studios, and teams who need production-ready 3D assets — segmented meshes with PBR, exported as GLB, FBX, OBJ, or USDZ. It is not CAD.
