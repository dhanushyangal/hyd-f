Hydrilla exports GLB, FBX, OBJ, and USDZ from BlueFox 1. Free includes GLB. Creator and Studio include all four. Pick the format your downstream tool actually opens.

## Which format should I use?

| Format | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| GLB | Web viewers, many real-time pipelines, glTF tooling, AR on the web |
| FBX | DCC interchange, Unity, Unreal |
| OBJ | Universal mesh import, simple DCC fallback |
| USDZ | Apple AR Quick Look and spatial handoff |

## What is different on Free vs paid?

GLB on Free. GLB, FBX, OBJ, and USDZ on Creator and Studio. Credits and seats are on [Pricing](/pricing).
