Hydrilla has three public plans. Free is $0 with 200 credits per month and GLB export. Creator is $9 per month ($7 per month billed yearly, 20% off) with 1,000 credits and GLB, FBX, OBJ, and USDZ. Studio is $25 per month ($20 per month yearly) with 4,000 credits, seats, REST API (10,000 calls/month), and all formats.

## When should I upgrade?

Stay on Free while you evaluate BlueFox 1. Move to Creator when you need paid formats or more volume. Move to Studio when you need seats or API access.

## Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans can be upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled from account settings.
