Export FBX from Hydrilla — that is the usual Unreal handoff — or GLB if your pipeline prefers it. Import into the Content Browser, verify PBR maps under a real light, then place the actor. Hydrilla does not ship an Unreal plugin.

## What holds up in a level?

Props, set dressing, and concept meshes for previs. Nanite, LODs, and collision still belong to your Unreal pipeline.
