FAQ

Do credits expire?

Extra credit packs are valid for 1 year from purchase. Subscription credits are replenished every billing period.

What if I run out of credits?

Top up anytime with extra credit packs — from $12 (400 credits). Packs work with every plan, including Free.

Can I use the assets commercially?

Yes, on any paid plan (Pro, Max, Studio). The Free plan is for evaluation only.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

Monthly or yearly billing?

Yearly billing gives you the same credits at 20% off — Pro works out to $16/month instead of $20, for example.
