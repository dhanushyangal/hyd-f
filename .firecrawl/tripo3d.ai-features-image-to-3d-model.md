## FAQ

### How do I turn a photo into a 3D model with Tripo?

Upload a photo and Tripo generates a textured 3D model in seconds. No modeling skills needed.

Drop in a single photo, or add up to 4 reference views (front, left, right, back) for higher accuracy — you don't need all four, even front plus back improves geometry significantly. Tripo analyzes shape, proportion, and surface detail, then builds a complete mesh with PBR textures. You can choose triangle topology (500 to 50,000 polygons) or native quad topology (500 to 25,000 polygons) before generating, and go up to 2 million polygons with High Detail Model.

Works with photos, illustrations, sketches, and concept art. Clean backgrounds and a single centered subject give the best results.

### What kinds of images work best for Tripo's image to 3D?

Tripo accepts JPG, PNG, and WEBP. A clean background with one clearly lit subject produces the best geometry.

Photos, digital illustrations, sketches, and AI-generated concept art all work. Multi-view input (up to 4 fixed angles) reconstructs surfaces from actual reference data instead of inferring them, so it's the better choice for asymmetric objects or anything with detail on the back. Symmetry control applies automatically — no setup required.

Busy backgrounds, multiple subjects, heavy motion blur, or extreme perspective will reduce accuracy. Cropping to the subject before uploading usually fixes this.

### How does Tripo's image to 3D generation work?

Tripo's AI reconstructs 3D geometry from your image's structure and shading, then generates matching PBR textures. A base model returns in seconds.

The model infers depth, volume, and surface orientation from a single view, or triangulates actual geometry when you supply multiple views. Textures are generated as a full PBR set — BaseColor, Normal, Roughness, and Metallic — so the asset lights correctly in any engine. Adding 8K texturing, retopology, rigging, and animation on top typically takes a few minutes total.

Everything runs in your browser. No plugins, no local GPU, no installation.

### Can I refine the 3D model after Tripo generates it?

Yes. Every generated model can be re-textured, retopologized, segmented into parts, rigged, and animated inside Tripo.

Use AI Texturing to regenerate or restyle surfaces with global style transfer and precise local edits, Texture Upscaling to take maps from 1K up to 4K or 8K, Smart Mesh or Quad Remesher to convert triangles into clean quad topology, Segmentation to split the model into parts at three precision levels (Simple, Balanced, Detailed), and Auto Rigging to add skeletons and skin weights for animation.

Each step is optional and non-destructive — you can export at any stage.

### Is Tripo's image to 3D free to use?

Yes. You can generate 3D models from images on the free plan without paying anything. The free plan covers single-image generation so you can evaluate output quality on your own references before committing. Multi-view input and the higher-tier generation models are available to subscribers (Starter, Pro, Max, and Team). Free-plan models are public and licensed under CC BY 4.0; subscriber models are private. No credit card is required to start, and everything runs in the browser.

Yes. Models generated with Tripo are authorized for commercial use, and the copyright of generated models belongs to you. This holds as long as your source materials — uploaded images or text prompts — are free of copyright disputes. Free-plan models are public and licensed under CC BY 4.0. Subscribed users (Starter, Pro, Max, Team) get private models with full commercial authorization. Two limits apply: you may not use outputs to build products or services that directly compete with VAST's, and you are responsible for the copyright status of your source materials — Tripo does not assume that responsibility. Full terms at tripo3d.ai/terms.

### Are Tripo's models ready for 3D printing, or do they need repairs?

Export directly as STL or 3MF and print.

For multi-part prints, use Segmentation to split the model into printable components at three precision levels (Simple, Balanced, Detailed) before exporting, so you avoid supports on hidden faces. Texture Upscaling to 8K also helps for multi-color printing where surface detail matters.

As with any source file, check the model in your slicer before printing — especially for very thin features or unusual overhangs.

### Can I use Tripo's models directly in Unity or Unreal for game development?

Yes. Export FBX, GLB, or OBJ and drop the asset straight into Unity, Unreal, Blender, Maya, or Godot.

For real-time engines, generate with quad topology and set a polygon budget — 500 to 25,000 polygons for quads, or up to 50,000 for triangles. Clean quad topology means predictable edge loops and correct deformation at joints, so the mesh survives rigging and animation without a retopology pass. PBR texture sets import correctly, and rigged characters export as FBX with full animation data for Unity, Unreal, or Mixamo.

For hero assets that need more density, High Detail Model goes to 2 million polygons.

### What's the difference between High Detail Model and Smart Mesh, and how many polygons can I get?

High Detail Model is for visual fidelity; Smart Mesh is for clean topology. Pick the product line first, then set your polygon budget.

High Detail Model focuses on high-fidelity assets — up to 2 million polygons, optimized for rendering, visual art, and detailed 3D printing. Smart Mesh focuses on topology, built for game development and interactive pipelines, and returns a clean mesh in seconds. Within Smart Mesh you then choose the topology type and target count: quads run 500 to 25,000 polygons, triangles run 500 to 50,000. Quads give predictable edge loops and correct deformation at joints, so rigging and animation work without a retopology pass; triangles are more compact for props and environments that ship unchanged.

Match the budget to the destination: a few thousand polygons for a mobile asset, 25,000 quads for a game character that needs rigging, 50,000 triangles for a static prop. You can also remesh after generating, or convert triangles to quads with Quad Remesher.

### Do I need any software installed to use Tripo?

No. Tripo runs entirely in your browser — no plugins, no local GPU, no installation.

Upload an image, generate, and export from the same web interface. The full pipeline — generation, texturing, retopology, segmentation, rigging, and animation — happens server-side, so a standard laptop is enough. Exported files (FBX, GLB, OBJ, STL, 3MF) then open in whatever local tool you already use: Blender, Unity, Unreal, Maya, or a slicer.

There is also an API if you want to generate models programmatically instead of through the browser.
