## FAQ

### What is Tripo and how does it work?

Tripo is an AI-powered 3D model generator that creates high-quality 3D models from text descriptions or images in seconds. Simply type a prompt or upload a photo, and our AI generates a fully textured 3D model you can download and use immediately.

### Is Tripo free to use?

Yes! Tripo offers a free tier that lets you generate 3D models without any cost. You can create models with text-to-3D and image-to-3D features for free.

### How do I turn an image into a 3D model?

Upload any image — a photo, sketch, or concept art — and Tripo's AI will generate a 3D model that matches your reference. Single images work great, and multi-view images can produce even more accurate results. No 3D modeling experience needed.

### What file formats can I export?

Tripo supports all major 3D formats including GLB / FBX / OBJ / USD. This makes it easy to import your models into any 3D software like Blender, Unity, Unreal Engine, or send directly to a 3D printer.

### Can I 3D print models generated by Tripo?
