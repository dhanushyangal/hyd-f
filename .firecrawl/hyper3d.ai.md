## Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers – and more.

1\. What makes Hyper3D useful for 3D generation?

Hyper3D turns text or images into high-quality 3D assets with clean topology and PBR materials. Rodin supports popular export formats for games, 3D printing, AR/VR, product visualization, and creative production.

2\. What kind of image works best with Hyper3D?

For Rodin image-to-3D, upload a reference image. If convenient, choose a view that makes the subject easy to inspect. Resolution, framing, background, lighting, and subject isolation are optional capture choices, not submission requirements.

3\. Can I optionally use more than one image?

Rodin works from a single image without requiring a prescribed pose or camera angle. Multi-view input is optional and provides additional visual guidance when available.

4\. What can I export from Hyper3D?

Hyper3D supports common 3D formats such as STL, FBX, OBJ, GLB, GLTF, and USDZ. You can use STL for 3D printing, GLB/GLTF for web and AR workflows, FBX for game engines, OBJ for general 3D editing, and USDZ for Apple AR experiences.

5\. Can Hyper3D models be used in commercial projects?

Yes, Hyper3D can support commercial workflows depending on your plan and license. Paid and enterprise options are designed for professional use cases such as games, e-commerce, product visualization, advertising, AR/VR, 3D printing, and production pipelines.

6\. How can I improve the quality of my generated 3D model?

Rodin is designed to produce high-quality 3D assets from a wide range of source images, with clean topology and PBR materials. Clear references and additional views are optional ways to guide the result.

7\. Is Hyper3D only for artists, or can developers use it too?

Hyper3D is built for both creators and technical teams. Artists can use it to accelerate asset creation, while developers and studios can use API access and enterprise options to integrate AI 3D generation into applications, marketplaces, game pipelines, or internal production systems.

8\. What can I do after generating a model with Hyper3D?

Hyper3D's Rodin models support 3D printing, games, AR/VR, product visualization, e-commerce, concept design, marketing, and interactive web experiences.
