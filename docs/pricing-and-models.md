# Pricing and models

Monthly plans. Counts below are whole models one month of credits can finish. Leftover credits stay on the account.

| Plan | Price | Credits / month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 100 |
| Starter | $5 (₹500) | 900 |
| Pro | $12 (₹1,200) | 3,000 |

A text prompt makes an image, then the 3D model. That is 45 credits at the default (image 15 + model 30). An uploaded image skips the image step and costs 30.

| Plan | From a text prompt | From an uploaded image |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 2 | 3 |
| Starter | 20 | 30 |
| Pro | 66 | 100 |

High model quality costs 40 instead of 30. With the default image step that is 55 credits from a text prompt, or 40 from an upload.

| Plan | Text prompt, high | Upload, high |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1 | 2 |
| Starter | 16 | 22 |
| Pro | 54 | 75 |

An image on its own is 15 credits, or 30 on high. That is not a model.
