# Pricing and models

How a month of credits turns into models. How money, plans, and Slot hours are stored is in [PAYMENTS.md](./PAYMENTS.md).

Monthly plans. Counts below are whole models one month of credits can finish. Unused period credits do not roll into the next month — each period refreshes to the plan allotment. Bonus or promo credits carry across renewals until spent. Slot uses the Starter credit budget. Slot Pro uses the Pro credit budget. The hour limit is extra and does not change these costs.

| Plan | Price | Credits / month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 100 |
| Starter | $5 | 900 |
| Pro | $12 | 3,000 |
| Slot | $3 | 900, for 1 hour a day |
| Slot Pro | $8 | 3,000, for 2 hours a day |

A text prompt makes an image, then the 3D model. That is 45 credits at the default (image 15 + model 30). An uploaded image skips the image step and costs 30.

| Plan | From a text prompt | From an uploaded image |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 2 | 3 |
| Starter, Slot | 20 | 30 |
| Pro, Slot Pro | 66 | 100 |

High model quality costs 40 instead of 30. With the default image step that is 55 credits from a text prompt, or 40 from an upload.

| Plan | Text prompt, high | Upload, high |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1 | 2 |
| Starter, Slot | 16 | 22 |
| Pro, Slot Pro | 54 | 75 |

An image on its own is 15 credits, or 30 on high. That is not a model.
